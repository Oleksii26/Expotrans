What does privacy policy mean?

  • This document concerns personal information collected by Expotrans through its website and its management.

Collection and use of personal information

  • You can visit the Expotrans website without disclosing any personal information. However, if you want to purchase the company's products or use certain Expotrans services, you will need to provide some personal information - register an account, subscribe to the newsletter.

Collection and use of non-personal information

Expotrans may use this information to customize the advertising and content you see and to fulfill requests for certain products and services. However, Expotrans does not use this data.

Transfer and disclosure of information

Expotrans may disclose your personal information to third parties who conduct business on our behalf – providing Expotrans products and services. For these purposes, we exchange information with third parties. In doing so, we agree on a privacy policy.

Expotrans may disclose your personal information in cases:

  • When we have consent to share such information to achieve certain purposes;
  • When we need to respond to a court order or any other legal process;
  • We need to ensure the safety of our website users and protect the rights and property of Expotrans;
  • When we believe that your actions on the pages of our sites violate the rules for using Expotrans products and services.

User ID tracking

  • Expotrans uses Google Analytics to track and analyze site visits and performance. Google Analytics uses a unique user ID if the user is logged in under their name.

  • Expotrans will not upload data that allows Google to identify an individual (such as name, social security number, email address, or any other personal data) or information that identifies a specific device (such as IP address).
  • To avoid data collection in analytics, opt out of Google Analytics and install a special browser add-on. For more detailed information on installing and removing the add-on, see the relevant help resources for a specific browser.

Media advertising

  • Expotrans serves ads and uses remarketing through Google Analytics. This means that third-party resources, including Google, may display our ads on websites across the Internet.

  • If you want to opt out of advertising, simply configure the Google Display Network using Ads Settings or using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, which will ask the Google Analytics JavaScript not to send site information when visiting Google Analytics.
